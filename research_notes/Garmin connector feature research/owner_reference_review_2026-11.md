**Read 23 November 2026.** These sources help identify useful screen interactions,
validation questions and data access patterns. Product pages and tutorials do not
establish physiological thresholds or validate a personalized coaching score.

## Implemented in v0.17.0

| Observed workflow or method | What the dashboard and engine use |
|---|---|
| Charpentier shows the latest activity with a selector and lets the assistant query the same underlying activity series. His example question compares effort across repeated routes. [Article, example dashboard and Grafana MCP links](https://charpeni.com/blog/visualizing-garmin-data-and-building-a-personal-ai-training-assistant) | The run page gives a single matched-effort finding. The next screen shows the exact earlier efforts, their pace, moving time, climb and HR. MCP returns the same records. Shared route GPS matching remains a separate task. |
| Garmin forum participants describe duration-grouped rankings and complain that pace averages hide their individual runs. [Discussion](https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/garmin-connect-web/407430/analytics-analysis) | Match duration as well as pace, and show individual dated readings before suggesting a change. Use each athlete’s own median rather than rank or an app-wide cutoff. |
| Gneta’s cohort study uses morning Garmin snapshots, steady workout filters, at least 20 pairs per athlete and within-athlete Spearman ranks. It relates score to a speed/HR proxy. [Study, charts and methodology](https://www.gneta.app/research/training-readiness) | Use a verified pre-run timestamp and one qualifying workout per date. Test the recorded score against HR versus prior matching efforts. Show coverage and paired points. Our outcome and filters differ, so the coefficient cannot be directly compared with Gneta’s, and its cohort median does not determine the owner’s result. |
| FIT Dashboard’s shared telemetry views keep the underlying workout time and readouts connected. [Demo and telemetry documentation](https://github.com/arpanghosh8453/fit-dashboard#readme) | The offline run cursor shows optional environmental readings aligned by the same FIT clock. This adds no GPS map, extra numeric axis or provider request. |
| The upstream client documents activity weather but leaves its response raw. Taxuspt’s adapter documents that even a metric account receives Fahrenheit in `temp` and account-dependent wind units. The independent ALEAPP export parser shows `temp`, `relativeHumidity`, `issueDate` and station labels in cached records. [Upstream endpoint](https://github.com/cyberjunky/python-garminconnect) · [Weather unit evidence](https://github.com/Taxuspt/garmin_mcp/blob/a2ca5fcac34524b83814fe2fcf3d479f8a75983f/src/garmin_mcp/activity_management.py#L788-L868) · [Cached schema](https://github.com/abrignoni/ALEAPP/blob/770d58a9c8721b76eef173b1aa03c4c7bc25b692/scripts/artifacts/garmin.py#L236-L257) | Convert the known snapshot temperature field, keep relative humidity, and preserve the reference time as an upstream label. Omit Garmin wind until its units are known; allow wind in m/s from an explicitly unit-labeled CSV. Confirm real stored payloads on the Mac. |
| Training Readiness is a list of timestamped snapshots, not a single static daily value. [Upstream typed client](https://github.com/cyberjunky/python-garminconnect/blob/82bc11073b597d20f8b080b7aeaac8d4cef4fc3e/garminconnect/typed.py#L323-L340) | Pair the earliest timestamped Garmin record of the local date only when it precedes the qualifying run. Older exports without a time cannot establish the sequence. |

Synthetic tests cover future runs, unlike pace/duration/terrain, missing measurements, sparse
form evidence, exact HR differences, readiness recorded after a run, Fahrenheit conversion,
window boundaries, blank periods, zeros, and both AQI standards. Live validation against the
owner’s archive and measurement disagreement across providers remain in [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md).

## 24 November 2026 · Today flow and cross-domain references

| Reference | Observed public pattern | Application here |
|---|---|---|
| [Garmin Connect](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/garmin-connect/id583446403) | Short Home summaries lead to a dated measurement and its details; Sleep factors follow the result. Keep local evidence rules. |

Public screenshots and documentation establish presentation patterns. They do not test the
signed-in workflows or watch delivery. Independent review reproduced the skipped HTML build,
missing Garmin score failure, broken intraday History link and initially low weekly plan.
The release fixes those issues and checks the smaller phone layout using synthetic records.
