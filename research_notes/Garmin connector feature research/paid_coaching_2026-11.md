# Paid coaching review — 24 November 2026

Sources are public vendor documentation. The descriptions concern advertised features. Individual
availability can vary by device, region, and account.

| Reference | What the paid product offers | Local implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Garmin Active Intelligence | Optional personalized health and activity insights on the Connect app Home throughout the day. [Active Intelligence](https://support.garmin.com/en-MY/?faq=kWi5DoaMPZ4VCJBA0lFWP7) | Wake-time follow-up and a Sunday review link to the observations behind the findings. Local Ollama can compose cited measurement clauses. |
| Garmin Connect+ | Custom performance charts, training guidance, live activity and enhanced LiveTrack, nutrition logging, Trails, 3D maps, social features and annual review. [Connect+ overview](https://support.garmin.com/in-ID/?faq=awrd4J1Du94fqM3SxQ0biA) | Add a distance-aligned replay from existing GPS traces. Keep activity coordinates local and preserve the offline chart and publishing model. |
| WHOOP One | Sleep, recovery, strain, coaching, Journal, trends, strength tools and VO₂max. Journal links tagged behavior to outcomes. [Membership features](https://support.whoop.com/s/article/Membership-Features-Benefits?language=en_US) · [Recovery Impacts](https://support.whoop.com/s/article/Recovery-Insights?language=en_US) | Show baseline-adjusted sleep journal comparisons and rested ratings alongside Garmin sleep scores. |
| WHOOP Peak | Adds Health Monitor, Stress Monitor and Healthspan. [Membership options](https://www.whoop.com/in/en/membership/) | Use existing readings and the owner's recent step median. The data does not support reproducing a WHOOP age or stress score. |
| WHOOP Life | Adds regulation- and sensor-dependent Heart Screener, blood pressure insights and priority support. [Membership features](https://support.whoop.com/s/article/Membership-Features-Benefits?language=en_US) | These features require inputs and validation beyond the current archive. |
| WHOOP Weekly Plan | Goals and behavior tracking in a weekly view. [Weekly Plan](https://support.whoop.com/s/article/Weekly-Plan?language=en_US) | Match saved planned sessions to observed workouts and base the following week's suggestion on completed time. |

The owner can add context through local MCP tools. Automatic watch writes, notifications, nutrition,
live tracking and 3D terrain would each need distinct data or delivery work. They are outside this
release. A future notification concept is recorded in the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md).
